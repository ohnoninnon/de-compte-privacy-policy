Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dé compte handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Dé compte does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or password.

The game may store certain game preferences and progress locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us.

2. Advertising

Dé compte uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a service provided by Google LLC.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from your device, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location, and information related to advertising interactions. This information may be used for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and personalizing advertisements, depending on your consent and applicable laws.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use apps and websites that use its services, please visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Dé compte uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent where required by applicable privacy laws.

Depending on your location and applicable regulations, you may be asked to choose whether certain information can be used for advertising purposes.

You can also change your privacy and consent choices later through the privacy settings available in the game.

4. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob – advertising and ad measurement.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – consent management.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Children's Privacy

Dé compte is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

6. Data Retention

Game preferences and progress stored locally remain on your device until they are deleted by you or the application is uninstalled, unless otherwise specified by the operating system.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to their respective retention policies.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

You can also manage your advertising consent choices through the privacy options provided by the game.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Dé compte, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
